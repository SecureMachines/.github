## About us

SecureMachines is a security systems company focused on cryptographic primitives: HSMs, entropy engines, and secure silicon components that higher-level infrastructure relies on. We serve enterprise, government, and defense environments protecting critical infrastructure.

Our principle is simple: **build the hard parts in-house, stay standards-aligned, and design for certification pathways from day one.** That keeps our systems predictable under review, stable under integration, and resilient as algorithms evolve.

## Products

Three foundational primitives, usable as standalone systems or embedded as secure components.

| Product | What it is | Highlights |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Prastaara** | PQC Hardware Security Module (HSM) | ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA, Classic McEliece alongside classical algorithms; PKCS#11 interface; FIPS 140-3 ready / planned |
| **Augha** | Hardware entropy engine (TRNG) | NIST SP 800-90B compliant TRNG; hybrid TRNG + DRBG random bit generator per SP 800-90C |
| **Pingala** | Edge / network cryptography device | IPsec Layer-3 with post-quantum algorithms; plug-and-play upgrade for existing endpoints |

## Technology

We build the cryptographic and hardware primitives that other systems depend on, engineered for longevity, reviewability, and transition. Cryptography, hardware, and systems engineering are composed into a coherent trust fabric that stays stable as standards and threat models evolve.

- **Root of trust**: a stable hardware trust boundary anchoring identity, keys, and security-critical operations.
- **Post-quantum cryptography**: engineered into hardware from day one, with cryptographic agility for safe transition as standards mature.
- **Secure computation**: hardware isolation, controlled execution, and techniques such as FHE and MPC.
